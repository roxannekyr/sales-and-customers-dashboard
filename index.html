Project Overview

A dual-dashboard Tableau solution delivering executive-level visibility into sales performance and customer behavior. Built for data-driven decision-making, enabling year-over-year benchmarking across 4 core KPIs with a single dynamic year parameter.

KPIs Tracked

  • Total Sales (CY vs PY)
  • Total Profit (CY vs PY)
  • Total Quantity (CY vs PY)
  • Nr of Customers (CY vs PY)
  • Sales per Customer

Technical Features
